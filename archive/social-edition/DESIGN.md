# Barkley’s Morning — social edition

## Job and direction
Barkley’s QR tag must let a finder call or text Carol immediately. The website also gives this specific dog a funny, confident voice and a beautiful short walk through his favorite place. The user approved the sunlit illustration and three posts, rejected every secret/hotspot, and requested a cleaner opening, meaningful scroll, better animation, and an Instagram-style album with captions.

## Composition
One continuous page with two compositions. At the top: oversized BARKLEY, then the newest fictional post with a real avatar and immediate Call/Text actions. The painting continues below; new transparent poppies move in the foreground during scrolling. The foliage leads into a deep-green clearing containing two earlier posts and a compact photo-profile grid. A quiet footer links back to the contacts. The full fifteen-item album opens through the grid or its own standalone page.

## Palette and type
Sky #ade0e4; forest #173d31; ink #14392d; tennis #e9f49c; warm white #f8f8e8; poppy #ef852e. Retain Bricolage Grotesque 800 for the oversized name, 600 for section display, and Manrope 400/600 for posts and actions. The compact social layouts earn their borders and profile details; there is no extra top navigation, mood label, pause control, discovery hint, or hidden scene UI.

## Signature motion
Scrolling is a gentle camera move: the landscape lags behind the document while the separate foreground moves faster and opens onto the social section. Barkley does not change pose or perform. A brief arrival breeze and small, direct paw-like feedback provide additional movement. Automatic decorative motion ends within five seconds. OS reduced motion quietly disables movement. No visible pause setting or old persisted pause state.

## Critique before implementation
The prior one-picture layout compressed the experience too far and forced personality into hidden actions. This composition gives the second screen actual content. Do not add follower counts, fake reply controls, social sidebars, loading introductions, or a full gallery dump to the main page. Preserve contact access before image and JavaScript loading. An Instagram-like photo layout is presentation of the existing local album, not a created external account.
